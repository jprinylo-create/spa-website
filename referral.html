STARTING PINT ASSOCIATES Referral Quick-Reference

Who to send my way

I give founders the kind of sales expertise usually reserved for the pipeline calls inside established sales teams. Most are building the plane while flying it. I help them stop betting revenue on skills they are still learning in real time.

Common themes to listen for

  • They drive and close every deal themselves
  • They can't hand sales off
  • They're surviving off their own referrals and network
  • They've hired and fired reps on repeat
  • Sales feels hard, or they think they're bad at it and hate doing it
  • They've tried all the gurus and still feel stuck Courses, books, podcasts, and none of it translates to their own deals

Bottom line They feel like they suck at sales, and they want to suck less. Until that changes, they're the ceiling on their own growth.

Which door fits

Group

The Starting Point room

  • Wants an ongoing expert in their corner, not a one-time fix
  • Would gain from seeing other founders' real deals worked in real time, not just their own
  • Feels alone in sales and wants a room of peers in it with them

Private

One-on-one coaching

  • Needs a guide on a regular basis, not a one-time fix
  • Needs help executing and someone holding them to it between calls
  • Needs more access, not more time

Probably not a fit: quick lead-gen hacks, done-for-you agencies, companies just hiring a VP of Sales, employees who don't own the number, or pre-revenue with nothing to sell yet.

How to refer

No need to pitch, explain the program, or quote a price. Just say, "I know someone who has lived exactly this. Want me to introduce you?" If they say yes, connect us over email or text. I'll take it from there and find them the right door.
